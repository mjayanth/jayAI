How I work

Start with an outcome.
Build, evaluate and improve.

I prefer seeing a working version to talking around an idea. A small build makes the decisions and the gaps easier to see.

01 / Understand

What does a good result look like?

I start with the person doing the work. What are they trying to finish? Where do they lose time? What must be right every time?

Make it concrete

The current workflow, a real example, an accountable owner, and the conditions for accepting the result.

See the product approach

02 / Build

Make the idea visible.

I build a narrow version with the people who will use it. Their reaction to a working example tells me more than agreement with a proposal.

Crawl, walk, run

Test one useful task first. Expand after the team understands where it works, where it fails and what it costs to support.

See the roadmap

03 / Evaluate

Agree the checks before expanding.

For some tasks, a useful draft is enough. For others, completeness, accuracy and timeliness are the job. I define the checks around that difference.

Keep the evidence

Test the inputs, the work and the result. Record failures, correction effort and cost. Stop when the workflow cannot meet its acceptance criteria.

See evals and observability

04 / Review

AI does the preparation. People make the call.

I want agents to understand the workflow, prepare the work and reduce the heavy lifting. People retain judgment and authority over consequential actions.

Explicit sign-off

Show the evidence and the exact proposed action. Check the approver’s authority and the required process approvals before execution.

See governance and controls

05 / Improve

Trust grows when the results hold up.

Adoption is a function of trust and value. I look for repeat use, less correction and results people accept. Failures give us specific work to do next.

Close the loop

Investigate the cause, change the right part of the system and test it again. A loop needs a stopping rule and an owner.

See adoption and operations
